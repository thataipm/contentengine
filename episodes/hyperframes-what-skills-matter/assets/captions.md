A question I keep sitting with as I build more with AI: if it does the work faster than I ever
could, what am I actually still good at?

It's not passing checks. AI can clear every test you throw at it and the output can still feel
hollow. It's not correctness either, something can be written with nothing technically wrong
in it and still not sound like you wrote it.

What's left is judgment. The ability to tell what's real from what's convincing, what's good
from what merely passes, and what's actually yours versus what's just correct.
